# Bobber Buddy

**One key to fish.** Bobber Buddy equips your pole and fishing gear, uses fishing-buff food, applies your best lure, casts, and reels in when the bobber splashes, all from the same key. It also makes the splash easier to hear and logs every catch.

Built for **WoW Forever**, and it also works on Retail.

---

## Features

- **One-key fishing**: each press does the next thing needed: pole and gear, buff food, lure, cast. Press again when the bobber splashes to reel in.
- **Fishing gear and food**: hats, boots, gloves, rings, trinkets and fishing-buff food are recognised from their tooltips, so new or Forever-only items just work.
- **Swap back**: one key (or `/bb gear`) puts your normal gear and weapons back on.
- **Hear the bobber**: while you fish, music, ambience and dialog go quiet, sound effects go up, and the volume is raised by an amount you choose.
- **Mute the noise**: optionally silence crafting sounds (that anvil!), spell sounds, footsteps and voices while fishing. Bobber sounds are never muted.
- **Session tracker**: catches with icons and tooltips, casts, time, session value and **gold per hour**.
- **Fishing log**: every fish you've ever caught, how many and where, with search, sorting and time spent fishing by zone. Only your own fishing loot counts, never bought items.

## Auctionator integration

If you use **Auctionator**, session value and gold per hour use its auction prices. Without it, Bobber Buddy uses vendor prices.

---

## How to use

1. Open **Options > AddOns > Bobber Buddy** and set your fishing key (or use **Key Bindings > Bobber Buddy**).
2. Stand by water and press the key. Keep pressing it: it gears up, eats, applies a lure and casts.
3. When the bobber splashes, press the key again to reel in.

## Slash commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/bb config` | Open the options |
| `/bb log` | Open your fishing log |
| `/bb gear` | Swap back to your normal gear |
| `/bb toggle` | Show or hide the session tracker |
| `/bb stats` | Print your fishing statistics |
| `/bb minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
